# Support

* **Questions and ideas:** open an issue with the form that fits.
* **The node does not answer or the panel does not load:** say which board you have (s3-eth or s3-wifi), what the USB console prints and what the page Log of the panel shows.
* **Protocol questions:** see `docs/PROTOCOL.md` first. If you have a logic analyser or a serial capture of a control unit talking to its PC, that is the most useful thing you can share.

This is a one-person project and the node is at its first steps: answers can take a few days.
